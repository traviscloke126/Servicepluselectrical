For the owner: two ways to make the video

Which version do you want?

The layout above works with either one. This section is only here for the concept and comes off the page before launch.

Recommended
[Image: the owner filmed on a phone, standing next to an electrical panel]

Option 1: Real clips of you

You film short answers on your phone, about 20–45 seconds each, one per question. Visitors tap a question and your answer plays.

  • Cost: Nothing. No new accounts or subscriptions.
  • Effort: One afternoon filming 5–6 clips. Re-shoot any answer whenever you like.
  • Trust: Customers see the real person who'll show up at their house.
  • Fits your values: It's education, in your own words.
  • Hosting: Unlisted YouTube videos, or stored with the site.
Alternative
[Image: an AI-generated talking version of the owner, made from a photo]

Option 2: AI avatar from a photo

An AI service (such as HeyGen or D-ID) turns a photo of you, plus your voice or a stand-in voice, into a talking video. Some versions can answer questions live, like a chatbot.

  • Cost: Paid monthly subscription. The live, chatbot-style version costs more.
  • Effort: No filming. You supply a photo, a voice sample and the written answers.
  • Trust: Results can look slightly off, and some customers notice.
  • Your sign-off: It uses your face and voice saying things you didn't record.
  • Live answers: Hooking up ChatGPT needs a small server to keep the account key private. Basic GitHub hosting can't do that alone.
