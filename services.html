Troubleshooting & Repair

Electrical problems rarely announce their real cause. We test methodically until we find it, then fix the source instead of the symptom.

  • Breakers that trip repeatedly
  • Dead or intermittent outlets and switches
  • Flickering or dimming lights
  • Buzzing, warm or discolored outlets and cover plates
  • Partial power loss

Know the signs

When to call an electrician

If you notice any of these, don't wait on it.

  • Burning smell or scorch marks at an outlet, switch or panel
  • Warm or buzzing outlets, switches or cover plates
  • Breakers that trip again and again
  • Flickering lights when appliances turn on
  • Two-prong outlets or a fuse box in an older home
  • Shocks or tingles from appliances or fixtures

Smell smoke or see sparks? Shut off power at the main if it's safe and call right away.
